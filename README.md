## The rant

> The person who prints 'Resealable' on shredded cheese bags is running a psychological operation on me. I pull the little tear notch and it rips diagonally straight through the zipper track, every single time, like the bag was designed by someone who personally hates me. Now half my fridge is limp mozzarella pouches held shut by binder clips and prayers.

355 characters — under the 400-character cap, every sentence earning its place.
The judge reads rants two at a time and picks the one whose anger feels shared
rather than performed: this one is first-person, specific, and ends on the joke.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the rant to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else: no network,
no files read, no secrets. Run it with `python3 main.py`.

## Why this angle

The brief's own examples (broken printer, slow bus, Monday morning) are the
angles thousands of entries will reuse. A "Resealable" bag that destroys its
own zipper is the same everyday frustration, but fresh: one concrete detail
(the tear notch ripping diagonally through the track) and one image at the end
(mozzarella pouches held shut by binder clips and prayers).
